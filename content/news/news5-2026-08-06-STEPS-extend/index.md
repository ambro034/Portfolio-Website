The Science and Technologies for Phosphorus Sustainability (STEPS) Center, headquartered at NC State University, has been awarded nearly $22.5 million in follow-on funding by the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF). Graham Ambrose helps lead center efforts exploring local governance of phosphorus through Soil, Water, and Conservation Districts (SWCDs).
